## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
KANAKANJI is an offline stroke-practice app for anyone learning to read and
write Japanese — from your first hiragana to all 2,136 jōyō kanji used in
everyday Japanese.

WHAT YOU CAN PRACTICE
• All 92 kana — every hiragana and katakana character
• All 2,136 jōyō kanji — the official list taught through Japanese
  secondary school, organized by grade level (1–6) plus the remaining
  general-use kanji
• On'yomi and kun'yomi readings, meanings, and common example words for
  every kanji, sourced from the same dictionaries used by professional
  Japanese-English dictionary apps

HOW YOU LEARN
• Stroke-order tracing — watch a character's real stroke order animate,
  then trace it yourself with your finger or mouse. Get corrected
  immediately if a stroke is out of order or the wrong shape.
• Practice sets — work through hiragana, katakana, or kanji grouped by
  school grade, at your own pace.
• Spaced repetition review — characters you've practiced come back for
  review right when you're about to forget them, so study time goes
  toward what actually needs reinforcing instead of characters you
  already know cold.
• Progress tracking — see your mastery level for every character and
  category at a glance, plus your overall completion rate.
• Search and browse — look up any kana or kanji directly by character,
  reading, or English meaning.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
KANAKANJI works entirely on your device:
• 100% offline after installation — no internet connection needed to
  study, no loading spinners waiting on a server
• No account, sign-up, or login of any kind
• No ads
• No analytics, trackers, or data collection of any kind — your practice
  history never leaves your device
• No in-app purchases

Full details are in the in-app Privacy page and this listing's Data
safety section — both describe exactly the same thing, because there's
nothing more to disclose.

LANGUAGES
The app interface is available in English, Spanish (Español), and
Simplified Chinese (中文). Kanji readings and meanings are shown
consistently across all interface languages.

WHO IT'S FOR
Whether you're a complete beginner starting with あ、い、う、え、お or
studying toward JLPT N2/N1 kanji, KANAKANJI gives you a focused, quiet
place to build real stroke-order muscle memory — no gamified streaks to
maintain, no social feed, no subscription. Just characters, strokes, and
steady practice.

Character stroke data and dictionary entries are drawn from established
open Japanese-language resources (animCJK, the hanzi-writer-data-jp
project, KANJIDIC2, and JMdict); full attribution and license details are
listed on the in-app Credits page.
```
